# Aurora Luxe Travel — Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Ultra-high-net-worth individuals and affluent travelers considering bespoke concierge travel services. They browse aspirationally — often in the evening on high-end devices (MacBook Pro, iPad Pro, iPhone Pro Max). Budget ranges span $5K to $100K+ per trip. They compare against Aman Resorts, Black Tomato, Scott Dunn, and Four Seasons Private Jet. They expect discretion, authority, and white-glove service reflected in every pixel.

**Context**: Single-page marketing site. The job is to convert high-intent visitors into concierge requests. Secondary job: communicate exclusivity and build enough trust to justify $50K+ trip budgets.

### Brand Personality
**Three words**: Commanding, discreet, bespoke.

Not flashy (no Las Vegas energy). Not cold/clinical (no tech startup sterility). Think the lobby of a private members' club — warm stone, natural light, quiet confidence. Every element is intentional. Nothing is decorative for its own sake.

**Emotional goals**: Aspiration, trust, desire, exclusivity. The visitor should feel they've discovered a private door, not walked into a showroom.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: Light credibility-first — warm neutrals with navy and gold accents
- **Palette**: Warm ivory (#f5f3f0) base, champagne gold (#c9a76a) accent, deep navy (#1a3a52) for hero overlay / tiers / footer. Soft beige variants for section alternation (#faf9f7, #f0ebe5). Text in warm brown (#2c2620).
- **Tone**: Luxury editorial — think Monocle magazine meets a private members' club invitation
- **Anti-references**: Generic AI-generated sites (cyan gradients, glass cards, neon-on-dark, 3-column icon+title+desc), cruise ship brochures, overly minimal SaaS landing pages, any site that screams "template"
- **References**: Aman Resorts (restraint, materials), Rolls-Royce (authority), Monocle (editorial hierarchy), Aesop (warm minimalism), Four Seasons (light luxury, dark hero overlays)

### Design Principles

1. **Authority through restraint** — Every element earns its place. If removing it doesn't hurt, remove it. Luxury is editing, not accumulation.
2. **Warmth in light** — The palette should feel like linen and sandstone, not sterile white. Tint everything warm. Navy provides dramatic contrast at key moments (hero, tiers, footer).
3. **Editorial hierarchy** — Treat each section as a magazine spread. Vary compositions, break grids, use asymmetry. No two sections should feel structurally identical.
4. **Accessible luxury** — WCAG AA minimum. Reduced motion support. Touch-friendly (44px targets). Luxury that excludes people with disabilities isn't luxury, it's negligence.
5. **Memorable through craft** — Typography, color nuance, spacing rhythm, and motion timing create distinction — not gimmicks, gradients, or glass effects.

### Typography
- **Heading font**: Space Grotesk — geometric, confident, modern luxury
- **Body font**: Inter — clean readability at all sizes
- **Fluid scale**: clamp()-based fluid type from sm to 2xl
