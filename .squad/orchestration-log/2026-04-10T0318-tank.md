## Critical Findings

### P0 — Sections Invisible Below Hero

**Root Cause:** Framer Motion `AnimatedSection` and card variants use `initial={{ opacity: 0 }}` with `whileInView` triggers. On static/full-page renders (no scroll events), IntersectionObserver never fires — content stays at opacity 0.

**Evidence:** `full-page.png` shows blank dark sections after Hero. Individual section screenshots (with `scrollIntoViewIfNeeded()`) show content correctly.

**Impact:** SEO crawlers see empty page below hero. Users on slow connections or with JS disabled see blank sections. SSR fails to render visible content.

**Recommendation:** Change animation `hidden` states to `{ opacity: 1, y: 20 }` instead of `{ opacity: 0 }`. Content always visible; animations are progressive enhancement only.

**Assigned To:** Trinity (Implementation)

### P1 — Section Headings Clipped by Sticky Navbar

**Root Cause:** No `scroll-margin-top` or `scroll-padding-top` to account for navbar height (~80px).

**Evidence:** Screenshots show headings partially or fully behind navbar: "Curated Destinations", "Signature Experiences", "Membership", "What Our Members Say", "Design Your Journey".

**Recommendation:** Add `scroll-margin-top: 5rem` on sections and `scroll-padding-top: 5rem` on `html`.

**Assigned To:** Trinity (Implementation)

### P1 — Footer Brand Text Missing

**Root Cause:** AURORA LUXE logo/text not rendering or positioned outside visible area.

**Evidence:** `section-footer.png` shows nav links and copyright only.

**Status:** Observed but lower priority than P0/P1 visual issues.
